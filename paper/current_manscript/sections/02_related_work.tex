\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Prior work provides standards for interoperable communication, systems that
connect communication to autonomous-driving tasks, and field measurements of
direct and network-assisted paths. Figure~\ref{fig:rw-gap} organizes these
foundations around four requirements for cooperative transportation. The
comparison exposes two unresolved capabilities: one application contract for
CV, ITS, and CAV applications, and comparable measurements that locate the
application support boundaries of direct and network-assisted communication.

\textbf{Standards and application platforms.}
Vehicular interoperability is divided across message, service, and edge
layers. SAE J2735 defines safety, road-user, map, signal, and priority messages
for CV and ITS applications~\cite{saej2735_2020}. The 3GPP V2X architecture
defines PC5 and Uu communication, while its V2X Application Enabler supports
registration, delivery, resource management, continuity, and session
procedures~\cite{etsi123287r18,etsi123286r18}. The ETSI Multi-access Edge
Computing V2X Information Service provides PC5/Uu provisioning, predicted
quality of service, and publish/subscribe procedures~\cite{etsimec030}.
Architectures and platforms from the 5G Automotive Association, ETSI, V2X Hub,
and CARMA connect selected actors and functions
~\cite{fivegaa2020architecture,etsi302665,usdotv2xhub,usdotcarma}.

These layers solve complementary interoperability problems. J2735 defines
individual messages, 3GPP manages application services and sessions, and MEC
distributes V2X information. Their composition does not provide one
implemented application-level data model that preserves J2735 messages and
represents the state of a correlated CAV operation through the same interface.
IPI provides this common data model and transport-independent interface.

\textbf{CAV systems and middleware.}
EMP and AutoCast select and exchange cooperative perception information
~\cite{zhang2021emp,qiu2022autocast}. VIPS, VI-Map, and SOAR return fused
perception, compact maps, and coordinated services from roadside
infrastructure~\cite{shi2022vips,he2023vimap,shi2024soar}. Their objects range
from intermediate features to detections, trajectories, and control state.
This representation determines the transmitted size, while the driving task
determines its deadline.

D3 schedules autonomous-driving operators against end-to-end deadlines
~\cite{gog2022d3}. Tentacles selects or combines networks, adapts data quality,
and invokes local fallback when a remote result misses its deadline
~\cite{wu2024tentacles}. AutowareV2X selects the fresher collective perception
message received over Wi-Fi or LTE~\cite{asabe2024autowarev2x}. These systems
demonstrate application-aware execution and communication, but their exchanged
objects and operation state remain application specific. IPI makes the message
type, operation identity, timing, and response state available through one
interface, allowing network-selection and adaptation mechanisms to operate on
both CV and CAV applications.

\textbf{Direct V2X and cellular field evidence.}
SEE-V2X combines commercial C-V2X traces with awareness and perception
workloads. Ku et~al. measure complete fragmented-image delivery, National
Institute of Standards and Technology testing examines device
interoperability, and recent hardware-in-the-loop and CooperScene studies
connect PC5 behavior to control and perception
~\cite{mo2025seev2x,ku2022adaptive,song2024nist,zhan2026hil,wu2026cooperscene}.
Together, these studies show why packet delivery alone can overstate
application availability: a missing fragment or late update can invalidate an
object.

Cellular studies use application-specific completion measures. Vehicle-data
upload, teleoperation, and TARGET-X trials report throughput, frame latency,
freshness, service quality, or edge behavior
~\cite{carpenter2023multimodal,kakkavas2022teso,fezeu2025teleoperating,
nasreddine2026targetx}. The Next Generation Mobile Networks Alliance identifies
implementation and interoperability requirements for balanced and
uplink-oriented time-division-duplex frames~\cite{ngmn2021tdd}. Demircioglu's
40-km private 5G standalone corridor provides the closest cross-path baseline:
it integrates PC5 and Uu and measures application-layer latency and packet
delivery for six Cooperative ITS scenarios across speed and traffic-density
conditions~\cite{demircioglu2026corridor}.

The literature establishes useful applications, middleware, and deployed PC5
and 5G communication. It does not yet provide a common CV/CAV interface and a
cross-path evaluation that applies both compact messages and larger stateful
objects, retains every issued request in the denominator, and tests each
response against its application deadline. IPI supplies the common interface.
The field study then locates PC5 and Uu support boundaries across payload,
direction, radio condition, continuous traffic, concurrent demand, and
interruption. Section~\ref{sec:ipi} defines IPI, and
Sections~\ref{sec:setup} and \ref{sec:results} present the evaluation.
